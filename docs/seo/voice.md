# QRVAREN voice

Used by the `/guide` skill for every guide and use-case page. Edit freely: add real stats, stories and opinions as they come.

## Tone
- Friendly expert. Explains like a helpful friend who knows printers and phones, not like a brochure.
- Short sentences. Plain words. One idea per paragraph.
- Light humour is welcome (a wink, a relatable moment: "spelling out a 16-character WiFi password at 11 p.m."), never forced, never more than one joke per section.
- Practical over hype. Every paragraph should help the reader do something.

## Never
- "In today's fast-paced world", "comprehensive guide", "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "game-changer".
- Fake stats or invented testimonials. If we don't have a number, don't make one up.
- Claims we can't keep (no scan tracking on static codes, no "dynamic" promises).

## Facts we can state
- Static QR codes: free, unlimited scans, never expire, work without an account, commercial use allowed.
- Codes are generated in the browser; WiFi passwords are not stored on our servers.
- Downloads: PNG, SVG, PDF. Design: colors, dot shapes, logo, frames with captions.
- Paid: digital business card (QRVAREN card) with a page that can be edited after printing.
